About this page. The Charlie Dallas Lancaster Foundation is a fundraising charity. We don't provide counselling or crisis support ourselves. Every service listed here is run by another organisation, and the website links take you to their sites.

Adults

Staying Safe

If you're having suicidal thoughts, Staying Safe can help you get through right now and shows you how to make a safety plan for yourself or someone else.

Visit the Staying Safe website (external site)

CALM (Campaign Against Living Miserably)

Confidential, anonymous and free support, information and signposting for anyone in the UK.

0800 58 58 58

Open 5pm to midnight, every day.

Visit the CALM website (external site)

Samaritans

A safe place to talk any time you like, in your own way, about whatever's getting to you. They don't judge you or tell you what to do; they listen.

116 123
Visit the Samaritans website (external site)

Children and young people

Childline

Free, confidential support for anyone under 19, about anything.

0800 1111
Visit the Childline website (external site)

YoungMinds

YoungMinds fight for children and young people's mental health, so that no young person feels alone and all can get the support they need, when they need it. Their website has resources for young people and for parents and carers.

Visit the YoungMinds website (external site)

York and North Yorkshire

York Mind

York Mind promotes recovery from mental ill-health, emotional wellbeing and independent living. They offer counselling, education, activities and services for different ages.

01904 643364

General enquiries line.

Visit the York Mind website (external site)

Menfulness

A men's social wellbeing group that supports men with their mental health through activities and counselling.

Visit the Menfulness website (external site)
