Fundraise for us

We run several events throughout the year, and supporters raise money in all sorts of ways, from fun runs to a 41-mile row.

Set up a JustGiving page

Go to justgiving.com/cdlf, click "Fundraise for us" and follow the steps to set up your own page. Share it by message, email and on social media to receive donations online.

Gift Aid is claimed through JustGiving, which adds an extra 25% to donations. Please ask people to tick the Gift Aid box if they're a UK taxpayer.

Walk A Mile a Life

Our largest event, every year from 23 May to 23 June. Log your miles with Charlie's army on Strava, on your own or with friends.
