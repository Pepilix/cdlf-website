Businesses can make a real difference to what we're able to fund. If yours would like to support mental health and suicide prevention in York and North Yorkshire, we'd love to talk.

Why partner with CDLF?

We're a fundraising foundation. As Charlie's sister Victoria puts it, "we then gift the money on to other mental health support networks and charities locally."

  • Make a difference close to home

    Support mental health charities and initiatives in York, North Yorkshire and beyond.

    Between June 2021 and 2023, more than half of the money we gave went to two York charities, York Mind and York SASH.

  • Bring your people together

    Involve employees in fundraising challenges, sponsored activities and A Mile a Life.

  • Show what matters to your business

    Demonstrate a genuine commitment to mental health and the local community.

    We publish each grant we make, with the date, the charity and the amount, so your team can see where the money went.

  • Be part of Charlie's legacy

    Help continue the work of a foundation established by Charlie's family and friends.

    CDLF was set up in 2021, in his memory. You'll be talking to the people who run it. Read Charlie's story.

The difference it makes

Money raised is given as grants to mental health charities and pays for support boards that point people towards help.

Persimmon Homes, 2023

Persimmon Homes gave the foundation £5,000 through its Community Champions scheme, after Charlie's sister Victoria applied for funding. The cheque was presented at York Minster on 23 June 2023, before the final leg of that year's A Mile a Life. Double Olympic champion Alistair Brownlee walked with Charlie's family and friends.
